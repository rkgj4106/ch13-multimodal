Script to Moving Video

Gemma plans the shots. Wan generates moving subjects and camera motion. FFmpeg joins the clips.

First test: 1 shot, 49 frames, 30 steps, Silent. First-time model download/loading can take a long time. This service needs its own GPU allocation.

Online narration sends generated narration text to Google's speech service. Longer narration slows the generated clip to match the audio. Silent mode has no external TTS. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face on first use.

AI-generated depictions may contain artifacts or historical inaccuracies. Separate shots may not preserve the same face or costume. Captions are added after video generation.
